Kojima is Not Real

Kojimbo

Hideo "Games" Kojima is presented as a Japanese video game director and designer. He is known on the internet for his bizarre behavior and many resulting jokes.

Scanning

One of Hideo Kojima's most notorious moments is asking to scan Stefanie Joosten's feet to ensure Quiet's model had highly detailed feet.

Many joke about this being Kojima indulging in his love for feet.

In reality it is a carefully planned attempt to normalize scanning people in order to monitor them.
